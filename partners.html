The Partner Model · Kuma Operating System (KOS)

Built for scale-ups, powered by proven operators.

Kuma runs a codified diagnostic and governance engine. It lands high-stakes mandates, isolates structural friction and places vetted veteran operators under one delivery framework, so every specialist works from the same method and the same evidence.

01 · Diagnostic Scan

Independent systemic triage

A Managing Partner leads the 14-Day Leadership Friction Scan. We map decision latency, unaligned incentives and board-to-executive gridlock before designing any operational intervention.

02 · Targeted Deployment

Specialised domain sprints

Once the friction is mapped, we bring in vetted domain partners from the Senior Advisor Network, such as go-to-market, climate-tech, executive mediation or product architecture, to lead a focused intervention track of 60 to 90 days.

03 · Unified Governance

Single governance standard

Every partner delivers through the KOS methodology. Clients get one standard of governance and faster executive decisions, and partners spend their time on the problem rather than on business development.

The Human Operator Moat

AI can model your org chart.
It cannot fix executive friction.

We build with, deploy and stress-test AI systems every day. We are technologists and operators who know software can simulate market scenarios, optimise workflow architecture and draft strategic roadmaps in seconds.

What code cannot do is sit across the table from two co-founders who have stopped speaking candidly, navigate the political fracture of a Series A or B inflection or align an executive team under acute board pressure.

We don't advise from academic distance or hide behind slide decks. Every Kuma partner has carried P&L accountability as a former CEO, CRO or C-suite executive. We embed directly inside the room to resolve the human friction and operational drag that technology alone will never fix.
