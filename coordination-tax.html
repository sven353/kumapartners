How this is calculated

The model assumes 46 working weeks a year and a 40-hour working week, which gives 1,840 working hours per leader.

  • Executive payroll = headcount × average annual cost
  • Paid for internal meetings = payroll × (meeting hours ÷ 40)
  • Coordination drag cost = paid for internal meetings × friction factor
  • Sunk executive hours = headcount × meeting hours × 46 × friction factor
  • Full-time leaders equivalent = sunk executive hours ÷ 1,840
  • Speed penalty = meeting hours × friction factor, shown per leader per week and as a share of 40 hours

The friction factor is your own estimate of how much meeting time adds no decision. It is the input that moves the result most, so test all three presets.
