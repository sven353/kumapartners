Selected work

three very different rooms.

Client details are anonymised. The group sizes run from a handful of leaders to a whole company.

Six leaders, two offsites, one direction.

A fully remote fintech infrastructure scale-up of around a hundred people had outgrown the way its leadership team worked. A first two-day offsite looked at how the leadership system was really operating and clarified roles and decision rights.

Eight months later, a second offsite agreed the three-to-five-year vision, the next year's priorities and the commitments each leader made in front of the others. The story they later told the whole company was built and tested in that room first.

  • 6 executives
  • 2 days, then 4 days
  • Eight months apart

Four offsites, eight months, one leadership team.

A European deep-tech company was taking its product into a new, highly regulated market while scaling its commercial team.

We ran four offsites with the C-suite and key VPs, each building on the last. The first set a shared baseline, the second stress-tested the commercial assumptions, the third turned goals into commitments with named owners and the fourth built a permanent operating rhythm for the leadership team.

  • C-suite and key VPs
  • 4 offsites
  • 8 months

The leadership team first, then everyone.

A distributed scale-up of around a hundred people from many countries had a new strategy. The top thirty met first and turned it into 90-day plans for each area. The next day, they presented those plans to everyone, and each team worked out how it contributes.

On the last day, the company agreed together what good work looks like. Everyone went home with a plan they had built, a commitment they had made and a standard they had agreed to hold.

  • Top 30, then around 100 people
  • 4 days
  • Many countries

Sample programmes

What a Barcelona offsite can look like.

One sample for each size of group. Every real offsite is designed around your team, so treat these as a shape to react to.

Sample programme 01

The Horizon & Founder Reset

2 days · 2 to 6 co-founders or a small ExCo · a masia in the hills

Are we still building the same company?

A private masia under an hour from Barcelona: one table, no other guests and long walks between sessions. Small teams talk more openly away from the city.

Before arrival

We speak with each founder alone about what the company needs next and what they haven't said to the others. The agenda is built from those conversations.

Day 1

Where the company is honestly heading, then roles, decision rights and the disagreements that keep coming back. Dinner at the masia, with no programme.

Day 2

What came up turns into decisions: who owns what and how the founders will disagree in future. It ends with the commitments each founder makes to the others.

They leave with a shared view of the next two to three years, clear ownership at the top and a short written agreement on how they work together.

Sample programme 02

The Strategy-to-Execution Offsite

3 days · 5 to 14 C-suite and functional leaders · Poblenou, then the Penedès

What will we stop doing so the strategy can happen?

Day 1 is in a private loft in Poblenou, close enough for everyone to arrive the same morning. Days 2 and 3 move to a winery estate in the Penedès, about 45 minutes away, where the change of scenery marks the shift from discussion to commitment.

Before arrival

We interview the executives, review the current plan and agree with the CEO which decisions the offsite must produce.

Day 1

The strategy meets reality: the assumptions behind it, the trade-offs nobody has made and where functions are pulling in different directions.

Day 2

Choices and priorities, including what gets cut. Dinner in the vineyard.

Day 3

Each leader turns the priorities into a 90-day plan with owners and dependencies, then presents it to the others for challenge.

They leave with agreed priorities, a written 90-day compact and a check-in date in the calendar.

Sample programme 03

The Company Momentum Offsite

4 days · leadership team, then 15 to 100+ people · one estate near Barcelona

Does everyone know what they own this quarter?

A single estate with a private room for the leadership team and enough space for everyone once the company arrives. Because nobody moves, the work from the first two days stays in the room.

Before arrival

We interview the leadership team and agree with the CEO which decisions the first block must produce. Then we design the session flow and team compositions for the company days.

Days 1 and 2

The leadership team only. They align on the strategy and the year's priorities, build the story they will tell the company and test it on each other until it holds up. On the second evening the rest of the company arrives.

Day 3

The leadership team presents the strategy to everyone and takes open questions. The afternoon is for the whole company to spend time together through an activity chosen with you, such as sailing, a cooking workshop or a walk in the hills.

Day 4

Each team works out how it contributes and what it needs from the others. After lunch, teams present their plans to each other and the company agrees what good work looks like here.

They leave with a strategy the leadership team owns, a plan every team built itself and a shared standard for the year ahead.

Practical questions

Before you get in touch.

Do you organise the venue and logistics too?

Yes, if you would like us to. We can take on the whole of it: the venue, the rooms, food, transfers from Barcelona airport and any activities, so that your Chief of Staff or People team can concentrate on the substance of the offsite rather than the spreadsheet. Some clients prefer to keep logistics in-house, and that works just as well. We then agree early on who books what, and we stay with the design and the room itself. If you are still looking for a place, executiveoffsite.es lists the venues we know around Barcelona.

How far ahead do we need to plan?

Six to ten weeks is comfortable. That leaves time for the confidential conversations with your team, for the agenda to settle and for the better venues, which tend to book up early. When something genuinely cannot wait, such as a founder conversation after a difficult board meeting, a shorter session in the city can usually be arranged within three weeks.

What does it cost?

There are two parts. Our own work covers the preparation, the agenda, facilitation in the room and the follow-up afterwards, and it is a fixed fee agreed before we start, so nothing changes once the invoice arrives. The venue, food, travel and activities are passed through at cost. Most engagements land between €15,000 and €150,000. Where yours falls depends mainly on the size of the group, the number of days and the venue you choose.

How do you handle tension in the leadership team?

Carefully, and mostly before anyone is in the room. We speak with each person individually beforehand, in confidence and under NDA, and bring what we hear together as patterns rather than quotes, so that nobody feels singled out. In the room itself the partners moderate directly. We would rather name a disagreement early and work through it than let it sit under a polite agenda for two days. The aim is a decision people can stand behind afterwards, even those who argued against it at the time.

How do you work with larger groups?

Group dynamics change once you pass about twenty people, so from that size we bring two lead facilitators. Plenary sessions, where everyone hears the same thing at the same time, alternate with focused work in smaller teams, each with a clear question to answer and something to bring back to the whole group. That rhythm keeps people involved and stops a company offsite from turning into two days of presentations.

How do you handle confidentiality?

We sign a mutual NDA before the first conversations with your team and before you share any strategy documents with us. What individuals tell us during the preparation stays with us. We use it to shape the agenda, and it reaches the room only as an anonymous pattern, never attributed to a person.

Which languages do you facilitate in?

English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese. Preparation interviews can take place in whichever language each person is most at ease in, which matters more than it might sound: people tend to be franker in their own language, and the meaning of a hesitation is easily lost in translation. Plenary sessions usually run in the team's working language.

What happens after the offsite?

Every offsite ends with a short written record of what was decided: the priorities, who owns each of them and when they will be reviewed. We send it shortly afterwards, while the room is still fresh in everyone's mind. Around ninety days later we come back to look at what has held and what has drifted, and to help the team adjust. On longer engagements we add reviews at thirty and sixty days as well.

Do you only work in Barcelona?

Barcelona is home, and within an hour of the city there is an unusual range of settings, from masias in the hills to vineyards in the Penedès and the coast. We also travel wherever a team needs to be, and have facilitated offsites across Spain, Morocco, the Caribbean, France, Germany and the UK. The preparation and the people stay the same wherever the room happens to be.

Can our Head of People or Chief of Staff be involved?

Yes, and it often helps. We agree the division of work in advance. As a rule we take the harder strategic trade-offs and any mediation, while your own people lead the sessions on culture or functional topics that they know better than any outsider could. Their involvement also makes it easier to keep the momentum going once we have left.

What if our dates move?

Calendars in growing companies move, and we plan for it. Our own fees can be rescheduled flexibly up to thirty days before the offsite. Venue bookings follow the venue's own terms, but when dates shift we work to move any deposit to a new date within the same year rather than lose it.

Can you bring in an external speaker?

Where it adds something, yes. A short keynote can be useful at a larger leadership or company offsite, for instance to frame a shift in the market or in technology before the team gets to work. We draw on our own network of founders, operators and investors rather than the conference circuit, and we brief the speaker beforehand so that the talk feeds directly into the sessions that follow. For a small founding team we rarely recommend one.
