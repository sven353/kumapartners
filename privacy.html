Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data controller

The data controller responsible for the processing of personal data described in this policy is:

  • Kuma Partners
  • Lisbon, Portugal
  • Email: privacy@kumapartners.eu

For any question about this policy or about how your data is handled, contact us at the address above.

2. What personal data we collect

We collect personal data only where it is necessary to respond to your enquiry or deliver our services. This includes:

  • Contact form submissions: full name, work email address, company name, company website, headcount and funding stage, and the primary challenge you describe.
  • Diagnostic intake data: where you engage us for a Friction Scan, Alignment Sprint, or Strategic Advisory retainer, we collect information shared during confidential executive interviews, board materials you provide, and related engagement documentation.
  • Scheduling data: if you book a call through a scheduling tool, we collect your name, email address, and the meeting details you provide.
  • Technical and analytics data: aggregate, anonymized traffic data collected via cookies you have consented to (see our cookie banner and Section 6 below).

3. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data under the following legal bases, in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Legitimate interest: responding to enquiries submitted through our contact form, and maintaining the security and functioning of our website.
  • Contract performance: processing data necessary to deliver a Friction Scan, Alignment Sprint, or Strategic Advisory engagement once a mandate is agreed.
  • Consent: non-essential analytics cookies, which are only set once you accept them via our cookie banner.

4. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes it was collected for:

  • Contact form enquiries that do not lead to an engagement: retained for up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Client and engagement data: retained for the duration of the engagement plus 7 years, in line with standard commercial and tax record-keeping obligations.
  • Analytics data: retained in aggregate, anonymized form and not linked back to an identifiable individual.

5. Third-party data processors

We share personal data with a limited number of third-party processors who help us operate our business, each bound by data processing agreements consistent with GDPR:

  • Hosting: our website is hosted by Netlify, Inc.
  • Scheduling: meeting bookings may be processed by a calendar scheduling provider (such as Cal.com or HubSpot) once a booking link is active on this site.
  • Email: our business email provider processes messages sent to our contact addresses.

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

6. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies required for the site to function, and, only with your consent, aggregate analytics cookies that help us understand overall traffic patterns. You can choose "Essential Only" or "Accept All" via the cookie banner shown on your first visit, and your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request erasure of your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@kumapartners.eu.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
