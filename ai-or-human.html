Initiate a 14-Day Scan
The decision map · 4 minutes · result on screen

What should AI decide in your company, and what must stay human?

Most companies automate tasks and leave the decisions where they were. The work then queues in front of the few people who still sign off, and you pay for the wait in burn and runway. Map your recurring decisions and see what AI can run, what it should prepare and what only your leadership team can own.

Map my decisions →
  1. Zone 1

    AI runs it

    Frequent, easy to undo and based on data you already have. A person checks the exceptions.

  2. Zone 2

    AI prepares, a person decides

    AI does the analysis and the first draft. A named owner makes the call and answers for it.

  3. Zone 3

    Your leadership team owns it

    Hard to reverse, built on trust or on judgement that lives in people. AI makes this work more important.

        How it works

        How each decision is placed

        Each decision gets five answers: how often it comes back, how easily it can be undone, whether it rests on data or on context in people’s heads, whether trust, a relationship or a conflict sits at its core and who answers for the result.

        • A decision that is hard to undo, or that rests on trust or a relationship, stays with your leadership team whatever else is true.
        • The other answers add up to a score from 0 to 8. Seven or eight puts a decision in zone 1, four to six in zone 2 and three or less in zone 3.
        • When nobody clearly answers for the result, the decision is flagged: automating it would only move the problem.

        This is a structured self-assessment, not an audit. It reflects one person’s view, which is why the map is most useful when the leadership team draws it together.
