How the room works

Psychological safety comes first. Every participant signs a confidentiality pledge at the start: everything said during the offsite stays in the offsite. No recording, and no posting about what others share.

This offsite is also where Kuma Partners meets founders it may later invite to The Round, its year-round peer board. We say so openly. Nobody is assessed in secret, and we repeat nothing from the room. If you tick the box on the form, we talk to you about it afterwards.

Questions founders ask

Why should I take two days out in January?

January is when founders set the year, usually alone and in a hurry. Two protected days, away from the company, give you the time to decide what matters and what you stop, before the year sets the agenda for you.

What will I leave with?

Your objectives for 2027, written down, the things you have decided to stop and your first commitments. You also spend two days with founders who carry the same weight as you.

Why do this with other founders and not alone?

Peers who carry the same weight see your situation clearly and ask the questions you may have stopped asking yourself. Everyone signs a confidentiality pledge at the start, so what is said in the room stays in the room.

Who will be in the room?

Founders from the Norrsken community, mostly pre-seed and seed, and the two facilitators. Each date runs with at least 10 and at most 15 founders, and you have your own room.

What if I cannot spare two days?

Two dates are on offer so you can pick the one that fits your calendar, or tick both and we confirm one with the invoice. You can also start with the free year review and decide afterwards.

What is The Round, and what has it got to do with this?

The Round is the year-round peer board of Kuma Partners. This offsite is also a test for building it, and we say so openly. Nobody is assessed in secret and nothing from the room is repeated. Whether you hear more about it afterwards is your choice. Read about The Round.

When are the dates and where is it?

Thursday 7 and Friday 8 January, or Thursday 28 and Friday 29 January 2027, with one night. The place is in nature, one to two hours by car from Barcelona. We confirm the exact place once the group is set and organise shared rides with you.

What does it cost and what is included?

€990 + VAT if you register by 10 December. €1,450 + VAT from 11 December. The price is the same for both dates and includes your room, all meals and both facilitators.

Until when can I register?

Registration for 7 and 8 January closes on 18 December. Registration for 28 and 29 January stays open until 15 January.

How and when do I pay?

There is no payment on this page. After you register we email you to collect your invoice details, then send an invoice from Kuma Partners. The full amount is due within 7 days of the invoice. The last day to pay is 21 December for 7 and 8 January and 15 January for 28 and 29 January, because we need it to organise the place.

What if the offsite does not run?

Each date runs with at least 10 founders. We decide on 22 December for 7 and 8 January and on 18 January for 28 and 29 January. If an offsite does not take place for any reason, you get back everything you paid.

What if I cannot come after I have registered?

Cancel by 31 December for a full refund. From 1 January you get 50% back. You can hand your place to another founder at any time, free of charge.

What happens to my data?

Kuma Partners holds your registration details. With your consent we share them with Farid Chaouki for logistics and follow-up. See our privacy policy.

Registration is closed.
