      How it works

      How the score is calculated

      Each of the five core questions is scored from 1 to 5, so the total runs from 5 to 25. The execution friction index turns that into a percentage: 100 minus the total as a share of 25. Higher means more friction.

      • 5 to 12 points: critical execution drag.
      • 13 to 18 points: the scaling squeeze.
      • 19 to 25 points: a high-throughput operating system.

      The weakest link is your lowest-scoring dimension. The perception check flags a gap when you rate your confidence 4 or 5 while the first three questions add up to less than 10.

      This is a structured self-assessment, not a benchmark. It reflects your view of your company, which is exactly why the debrief starts by testing it.
