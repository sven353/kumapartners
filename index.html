The problem

What breaks when fast-growing companies hit pressure

01 · Velocity Drag

Decision Latency

Key hires, product pivots, and capital deployments stall because the leadership team avoids the hard call. Everyone can see the decision that needs to be made. No one is making it.

Impact: Burned runway and second-guessing at the board table.
02 · Latent Conflict

Co-Founder & C-Suite Fracture

Unspoken disagreements and eroded trust between founders or C-suite peers turn into political silos. Meetings get politer. Execution gets slower.

Impact: High-performer attrition and hidden operational drag.
03 · Structural Strain

The Scaling Bottleneck

The informal operating habits that worked at 20 people actively suffocate the company at 75+. The org chart expanded. Decision governance didn't.

Impact: Founder overload and missed milestone execution.
