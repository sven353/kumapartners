The problem

What breaks when fast-growing companies hit pressure

01

Decision latency

Key hires, product pivots, and capital deployments stall because the leadership team avoids the hard call. Everyone can see the decision that needs to be made. No one is making it.

02

Co-founder & executive fracture

Unspoken disagreements and eroded trust between founders or C-suite peers turn into political silos. Meetings get politer. Execution gets slower.

03

The scale bottleneck

The operating habits that worked at 20 people are actively suffocating the company at 100 or more. The org chart changed. The decision-making didn't.
