        Most leadership teams believe they are aligned on priorities. In our work with scale-stage teams, founders, COOs and functional VPs tend to feel friction in different places, even when they agree in the room.         The next step: the Kuma 14-Day Leadership Friction Scan
        1. Confidential 1-on-1 executive interviews. Structured diagnostic conversations with the CEO, the founders and each C-suite executive.
